What it reads

Everything the dish already knows

Starlink hardware answers a gRPC API on your local network at 192.168.100.1. DishWatch asks it directly, once a second, and keeps the history the dish itself throws away.

Signal and obstruction

One health score, the obstruction percentage, and how much sky the dish still has not mapped.

Throughput

Live up and down with peaks, taken from the dish's own counters rather than a speed test.

Power and energy

Draw in watts and accumulated watt-hours, so a battery or solar setup has real numbers behind it.

Outages

Dropouts counted as events — how many, how long the worst one ran, how much of the window was clean.

Power-bank tracking

Anchor your bank's capacity once and get state of charge and time remaining from measured draw.

History that survives

The dish keeps 15 minutes. DishWatch accumulates across reboots — and says so when a gap means it cannot know.

The CLI

macOS · Linux · arm64 & x86_64

sl prints a dashboard; sl watch gives a live terminal view. Useful over SSH, on a Pi, or in a script.

$ brew install faeton/tap/dishwatch
$ sl

Linux

The CLI runs wherever the dish is reachable

The menu-bar app is macOS-only — it is SwiftUI, and there is no honest way around that. The CLI is not: one static Go binary, no runtime dependencies, built for Linux on arm64 and x86_64.

That covers what this tool was really built for — a small always-on machine beside the dish, reporting over the network. Homebrew works on Linux, or take the tarball from the releases page. 

$ brew install faeton/tap/dishwatch   # macOS and Linux
$ sl watch                             # live terminal dashboard

Worth knowing

What it does not do

  • No account, no telemetry, no cloud. It talks to the dish on your LAN and to nothing else. The app is sandboxed and ships a restricted engine with no shell-outs and no third-party services.
  • It cannot see what the dish cannot. Speeds come from the dish's own counters, not an external test, so they reflect real traffic rather than a benchmark.
  • Fifteen minutes is the dish's memory. Poll less often and the gap is skipped, not estimated — and the readout says so instead of inventing an average.
  • Not affiliated with SpaceX or Starlink. An independent reader of a local API that requires no authentication.
