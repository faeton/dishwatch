Honest limits

What it will not invent

Candour is the brand. The watch reports what the dish can see, and stops there.

  • No account, no telemetry, no cloud. It talks to the dish on your LAN and to nothing else. The app is sandboxed and does not shell out.
  • It cannot see what the dish cannot. Speeds come from the dish's own counters, not an external test — real traffic, not a benchmark.
  • Fifteen minutes is the dish's memory. A gap is skipped, not estimated, and the readout says so instead of inventing an average.
  • The app is macOS-only — it is SwiftUI, and there is no honest way around that. The CLI is not, which suits the real use case: a small always-on machine beside the dish.
  • Not affiliated with SpaceX or Starlink. An independent reader of a local API that requires no authentication.
